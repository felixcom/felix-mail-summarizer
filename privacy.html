Privacy Policy

Felix Mail Summarizer is a personal desktop application used by a single user.

The application requests Gmail read-only access solely to read the authenticated user’s email messages for local summarization and search functionality.

The application does not:

  • send emails
  • modify emails
  • delete emails
  • archive emails
  • change Gmail settings
  • share email data with third parties

Email data is processed locally for personal use.

Users may revoke Google account access to the application at any time through their Google Account security settings.
